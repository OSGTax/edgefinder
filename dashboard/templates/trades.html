Trade history

Dates and times are the New York market clock (US/Eastern), matching the session a fill belongs to and the broker's own stamp. Corporate actions show no time — they are dated by their effective date, not by an execution. Profit is what a trade realized when it closed shares or contracts it already held, measured against the position's average cost rather than any one purchase. Trades that only opened a position show no profit. Current-era fills execute in a paper brokerage account at Alpaca, which charges no commissions and pays no dividends — open positions and any foregone dividend cash are not included here; the desk page has the whole-account number and the missed-dividends estimate.
